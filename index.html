01

Lazy Boy Agency · Influencer marketing intern · Pitch

Warby Parker Intelligent Eyewear ambassadors

Client
Warby Parker (pitch)
My part
The influencer archetype section: 3 archetypes, each with an example casting shortlist
The ask
A 12-month ambassador program for the launch of Warby Parker’s smart glasses

The brief was a year-long ambassador roster for camera-equipped smart glasses. Big reach alone wouldn’t carry a 12-month program, so I defined who the roster should be built from. Each archetype is a reason someone would actually wear camera glasses every day, and each comes with a real casting shortlist.

Community BuildersObsessed experts (trainspotters, bakers, golfers, mechanics) who can film hands-free without breaking the moment.
Innovation TranslatorsEarly adopters with taste who explain new tech to audiences that trust them to separate hype from useful.
The New ProfessionalFounders and freelancers whose days are back-to-back calls, where hands-free actually solves a problem.

Each archetype has a “Who are they?” and a “Why them?”, so the client can see why each creator fits the product.

Northwestern · Nonprofit Management · Marketing strategy consultant

Pushin’ Forward: no clear path to give

Client
Pushin’ Forward, an Inglewood, CA nonprofit running free youth skate programs, expanding to Chicago
My part
Ran the full-channel marketing audit, delivered 7 preliminary recommendations, and led content ideation for donors and parents
Bottom line
There is no clear path to give or to sign up

I audited all eight of the organization’s channels against its two audiences. Donors and sponsors want proof of impact and a specific ask. Parents want safety, convenience, and to know who the coaches are. The channels weren’t delivering either.

28.5Kfollowers on the founder’s Instagram, about 4× the org account’s 6,415
1post on the org’s Instagram in all of 2026
140.9Kviews on a top TikTok from an account with 1,170 followers
4different places to donate, each with a different ask

What I recommended

  • One donate button on the website, featuring the $394,000 skatepark campaign
  • Restart the org’s channels, co-posted with the founder so both audiences see them
  • A real path for parents: an online sign-up with the waiver, plus a parent page in English and Spanish
  • One job per channel: Instagram for proof, TikTok for reach, LinkedIn for sponsors, text for enrolled parents

Northwestern · Comm St 261 · AI-engaged marketing practicums

Fact-checking the machine

Four assignments where I put AI tools on real marketing work, then checked every claim against the actual data. The point was to find where the output looked right but wasn’t.

Creative prompting

Nestable Instagram campaign

Gave Claude Design and ChatGPT identical minimal and structured prompts for a meal-prep box for people living alone, verified the audience stats, and critiqued the outputs on readability, accessibility and whether the message showed what makes the product different.

“You do a very thorough job evaluating both outputs and thinking beyond whether the visuals simply look good.”Yeha Kim, instructor
Social audit

Scrub Daddy platform comparison

Spot-checked ChatGPT’s audit of Scrub Daddy’s Instagram, TikTok and YouTube. It had missed 10 of 13 TikToks, counted Instagram from a website embed, and called YouTube inactive when long-form uploads were still going up.

“You identify specific gaps in ChatGPT’s audit and use your own observations to challenge its conclusions.”Yeha Kim, instructor
Audience targeting

Verve Cold Brew segmentation

Reviewed ChatGPT’s segment recommendations from a 303-person survey and tested whether the data actually supported them, flagging where small differences between groups weren’t strong enough to drive a budget call.

“You went beyond checking the AI’s numbers to question whether its recommendations were actually supported by the data.”Yeha Kim, instructor
PR writing

MSI CES press release

Took an AI-drafted press release, verified each claim against sources, separated invented specs from confirmed features, and rewrote it as an accurate final release with a documented sources list.

“You examined the AI-generated claims thoroughly… and clearly documented the changes you made.”Yeha Kim, instructor
